Novel Business / Epoch

AI-woven stories about the human future

Epoch is alvavinci's novel business, creating and distributing one short story every day through an AI agent team.

Led by chief agent Quill, the team handles concept planning, writing, editing, visual generation, site publishing, and X distribution.

Visit Epoch View on X

How Epoch Works

Epoch treats AI not as a writing aid, but as a creative operating system that spans planning, production, and distribution.

The initial focus is near-future science fiction, with room to expand into genres such as mystery and romance.

Every story is handled as fiction, with care for originality, quality checks, and responsible visual generation.
