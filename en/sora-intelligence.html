AI Division

About the AI Division

Sora Intelligence builds AI agent teams that collect, organize, analyze, and report information for each business area.

The division helps representatives and operators focus on decisions by preparing the information and context before those decisions.

View Functions

Sora

Sora Intelligence's Approach

We treat AI as an execution layer that improves the quality of information before management decisions.

Each business area has different sources, viewpoints, and reporting needs. Sora Intelligence designs specialized AI teams around those contexts.

Core Functions

Information Collection

Collect market data, project information, news, and operating signals.

Summarization

Turn scattered information into clear summaries and decision-ready context.

Analysis

Support comparisons, trend analysis, and financial framing before decisions.

Reporting

Deliver ongoing updates through Slack, reports, and recurring operating loops.

AI Team Structure

Real Estate AI Team

Supports property research, investment screening, and hospitality or private lodging strategy.

Business Development AI Team

Supports deal research, succession analysis, operating improvement, and post-succession monitoring.

Finance AI Team

Supports financial monitoring and reporting.

Media and Novel AI Teams

Support media operations, IP development, and creative pipelines.

Operating Policy

  • Humans make final decisions; AI prepares the information before them.
  • Collection scope, level of detail, and reporting formats are tuned for each business area.
  • Freshness, assumptions, and risks are made explicit.
