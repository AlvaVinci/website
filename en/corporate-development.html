Business Development

Business development and succession

We approach business succession and strategic acquisitions as long-term operating commitments, supported by a business development AI team.

Rather than acquisition for its own sake, we respect existing strengths, employees, customers, and trust, while organizing deal information, initial analysis, and post-succession improvement.

View our approach
Philosophy

Our approach

§ I

We see business development and succession as the work of inheriting and growing a business, not of acquiring one.

Our stance is long-term. We look for the essential value of each business and support the conditions it needs to keep growing.

Existing operations, employees, and the trust built with customers are the parts hardest to see and easiest to break. We treat them as the core of the business.

Activities

What we do

Our activity centers on succession and strategic acquisition that contribute to the long-term growth of the group.

§ II
01

Business succession

We take on successions driven by retirement or the absence of a successor, respecting the history and value of the business.

02

Strategic acquisition

We acquire and grow businesses that contribute to the group, with the AI team organizing context and open questions.

03

Business expansion

We explore adjacent areas while preserving existing strengths, organizing market, customer, and operating context.

04

Partnerships

We consider capital, operating, and business partnerships where they fit the situation.

AI Team

Working with the business development AI team

The AI team organizes deal information, business context, financial and operating issues, and post-succession improvement themes on a continuous basis.

§ III
01

Deal research

Agents structure business profiles, customer bases, revenue models, organization context, and succession issues for comparison.

02

Initial analysis

Agents summarize market conditions, competitors, financial information, and operating processes before deeper review.

03

Post-succession support

Improvement is gradual — workflow organization, reporting, and ongoing monitoring rather than abrupt change.

Stance

How we operate

§ IV
  • We assume long-term ownership
  • We do not aim for short-term resale
  • Continuity of the existing business comes first
  • We judge flexibly, based on how the business actually runs
  • We support management and organizational structure where needed
Scope

Focus areas / Scale

§ V

Focus areas

  • IT / AI / SES / contract development
  • Small businesses across sectors
  • Businesses with a durable customer base
  • Businesses where AI and operational design can support continuity

Scale

  • Small deals are welcome
  • Revenue and profit scale are considered flexibly
  • From sole proprietorships to small and mid-sized companies
Process

How a conversation proceeds

We value substance over formality and adapt to each situation. Early-stage conversations are entirely welcome.

§ VI
01

First contact

Reach out at any stage. Everything is treated confidentially, including exploratory exchanges.

02

Information exchange

We build a shared understanding of the business and your intentions, and check whether the fit is real for both sides.

03

Review and proposal

We propose a structure that fits how the business actually runs — share transfer, business transfer, or another scheme.

04

Agreement and execution

We proceed carefully under terms both sides accept, working with intermediaries and advisors as needed.

After

After succession

After succession we continue to value employees, customers, and existing relationships, and work with the AI team toward sustainable growth without forcing the pace.

§ VII
  • We prioritize continued employment
  • We respect existing supplier and customer relationships
  • No abrupt change — improvement comes through workflow organization and monitoring
  • We continue operating on the strengths already there
  • We aim for growth on a medium- to long-term horizon
